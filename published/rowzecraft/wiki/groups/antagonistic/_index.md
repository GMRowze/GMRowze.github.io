## Religious Cults

The cults of Rowzencraft share little in common beyond their rejection of ordinary human limits. Some seek hidden truths buried beneath Creation. Others search the heavens for minds older than mankind. A few pursue power, immortality, forgotten gods, or stranger objectives still. Despite their differences, all have produced movements, conspiracies, and institutions that have repeatedly brought them into conflict with both Church and State. The largest cultic traditions are generally classified as:

- **[Archontic](archontics)** are Christian Heretics that seek hidden truths concerning the nature of Creation, the origins of mankind, and the powers believed to govern the cosmos from behind the visible world.

- **[Eldritch Cults](eldritch_cults)** devote themselves to ancient and incomprehensible powers believed to predate human civilization, seeking communion with forces that exist beyond ordinary human understanding.

- **[Infernalists](infernalists)** seek knowledge, influence, and power through association with demons, hostile spirits, and other malevolent entities from Christian theology.

- **[Neopagans](neopagans)** attempt to restore, reconstruct, or revive pre-Christian religious traditions, often centered upon local gods, ancestral cults, and forgotten rites.

## Modern Cults

Unlike the older [cultic traditions](../religious_cults/), the Modern Cults emerged from the scientific, industrial, and technological transformations of the nineteenth and twentieth centuries. Their adherents rarely regard themselves as mystics, occultists, or even cultists. Instead, they typically present their ideas as rational, progressive, and grounded in scientific inquiry.

Despite this self-understanding, critics have frequently noted the resemblance between these movements and more traditional cultic organizations. Both tend to organize themselves around a single transformative idea, demand extraordinary commitment from their adherents, and regard existing human institutions as temporary obstacles to a radically different future.

The most significant Modern Cults are generally classified as Alienists and Augmentists:

- **[Alienists](alienists)** pursue contact with non-human intelligences, believing that humanity's future depends upon communication with civilizations older, wiser, and more advanced than itself.

- **[Augmentists](augmentists)** advocate the deliberate transformation of humanity through science, biotechnology, cybernetics, artificial intelligence, and other forms of technological advancement. Many regard the human condition not as a permanent state, but as a problem to be solved.
