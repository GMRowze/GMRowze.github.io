## Anti-Cultist
### The Order
Active: 1600-1820
A sucessor order of the Templar Knights from Portugal and the inquistion. They were subtle and almost invisibile, but worked with cruel efficiency to stomp any cultist activity. The order was involved in a struggle for power after the coming of the Royal Court (and their royal cultists and esoterics), they declined shortly after the Napoleonic Wars and most of their libraries were destroyed except for a few manuscripts left in old catholic missions

## The Chevaliers
Active ~1600 in France 1850-1920 in Brazil.
A secretive anti-cultist french protestant organization. In Brazil it never surpassed 50 or so people of the french-imperial brazilian elites, the most famous member (altought not truly in the core) was Count D'Eu. They essentially functioned as a "power-behind-the-curtain" stopping what they considered heretical and cultists. Their elitism and few members eventually made their decline inevitable - this, togheter with some bitter infighting between the protestants and catholics inside the organization - eventually led to the gradual decline of their influence. By 1922, the few remaining members were driven off and most either died in obscureness or suicided.

### The Teutons
Active: 1225-1920; Reorganized in 1923; Corrupted in 1930-140
An inquisitorial organization within the Teutonic Order, first created in the Holy Land and then dedicated to rooting out Pagans in the Baltics before evolving to hunt witches in Germany. Known to their religious fanaticism, for much of history they closely resemble a cult themselves.


### The Engineering Battalion
Active: 1850-1860; 1900-1930; 1960
The 34th Pioneer Engneering Battalion, initially active in the Paraguayan War, this unit essentially was fundamental in Count D'Eu's final push into Paraguay and remained in the country for the reconstruction plans. There they had contact with the Spaniard Cult. From time to time, they would be used by the government in anti-cult actions - even thought they were not fully informed what they're fighting. Their actions in 1918-1930 include the burning of infected bodies during pandemics and in the 1960's they appear again isolated deep in the amazon jungle. However by this point they have srongly lost their past strenght.

### The Agency
Active: 1910-1940; Corrupted and destroyed in the 1950's.
A civilian organization originally called the Radio & Telegraph Agency in the 1910's. They were private radio scientists associated with the Rio de Janeiro radio and the universities. They eventually started to dwelve deep into the deep lore when they were realizing experiments on the battleships hunting german submarines during WW1. After the incident, they slowly started to advance science in a way to stop the cosmic horrors. By the early 1920's it was already completely a secretive-private agency that combated comisc horrors. In 1930's they suffered severe setbacks due to the economic crisis and the subsequent Vargas dictatorship. They were reactivated after the war and continued to operate until the mid-1950's, when most members eventually turned Alienists.

### Blackcove Detective Agency
Active: 1920-1930
A private detective agency created by Sir Henry Blackcove to solve crimes, specially disappearances whenever the police couldn't solve it. During their existence, they had many run-ins with cultists. After the mysterious death of Sr. Blackcove, the detective agency closed its doors but its most effective agents went to work with the Agency.

### Latvian Foreign Service
