### Summary

Set in 1898 in the remote forests of northern Courland, _An Open Grave in Edwahlen_ follows Detectives Martinsons and Gendarme Edgars as they travel west to investigate the murder of an elderly pastor in Edwahlen, an isolated village overshadowed by an old church and an unsettling reputation among the locals. What begins as a provincial murder inquiry quickly turns stranger when telegrams disappear, unseen figures stalk the detectives through rain-soaked medieval streets, and graves appear to open and close at night. As they move deeper into the countryside, Martinsons and Edgars uncover a web of superstition, old grudges, and buried pagan history lingering beneath the fragile authority of church and empire. In a land where graveyards and abandoned traditions seem to press constantly against the edges of civilization, the two detectives must determine whether the curse surrounding Edwahlen is merely fear, superstition and manipulation or whether something older and darker has taken root in the village

### Other Information
