The grave of Pastor Berthold Gottfrit von Bers was open days before he found himself with soaked in blod in the floor of his own church, dear brother. Just two weeks short of his seventy-first birthday, though he never marked it. He had been born into that church, and apart from a handful of years spent in Germany at a Lutheran seminary, he had lived his entire life within its walls. 

He would now spend eternity in the damp graveyard beside it.
