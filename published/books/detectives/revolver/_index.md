### Summary

Set in 1891 in the Ural Mountains of the Russian Empire, A Revolver for the Czar's Frontier follows the expansion of the imperial railway into a harsh frontier where mines, settlements, and scattered mountain villages stand at the uncertain edge of "civilization." After stopping the lynching of an accused railway worker at a remote construction camp, a young Latvian soldier named Edgars becomes caught between imperial law and the brutal logic of frontier justice. His investigation into stolen railway supplies and a mysterious outlaw hidden in the mountains gradually draws him toward a powerful industrial landowner and a campaign of intimidation against a pacifistic settler family living deep in the hills. As famine, unrest, and violence spread across the region, Edgars is forced to decide whether a lawful man should choose violence in a land increasingly ruled by fear, weapons, and the expanding reach of the Empire.
