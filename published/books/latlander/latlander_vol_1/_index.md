The silence of the dawn is broken by the drums of artillery. So it has been for the past two years in Latvia. When the Germans invaded the Russian Empire during the War to end all wars, they were expecting to find a quick victory in this region. Instead, they found foggy swamps and ancient forests, full of old legends and tales. In the land where trenches are dug besides viking burial mounds and ancient crusader battle sites, the germans found a people who are willing to fight to the end for their home.

Lieutenant Markuss is one of those whose village now lies behind the german frontlines, and he must now rush against time to find his brother who disappeared in battle. From the deepest trenches to snowy fields and foggy forests, Markuss' quest will lead him through this forgotten world turned battlefield.
