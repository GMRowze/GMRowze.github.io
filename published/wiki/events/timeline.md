## 1915

- **27/04/1915 (14/04 O.S.)**: German offensive into Courland begins. The 1st and 2nd Daugavgrīva Fortress Battalions participate in the initial defensive actions[^1][^2].

- **30/04/1915 (17/04 O.S.)**: Grand Duke Nikolai Nikolaevich signs the order for the deportation of Jews and Germans from Courland and Vidzeme. The measure affects approximately 60,000 people.[^3].

- **01/05/1915 (18/04 O.S.)**: Retreating Russian forces reach Jelgava[^1].

- **02–03/05/1915 (19–20/04 O.S.)**: Battles of Jelgava. Russian forces, including the Daugavgrīva Battalions, defend the city against the German advance[^1][^2].

- **04/05/1915 (21/04 O.S.)**: Russian counterattack forces German troops to withdraw from much of Zemgale[^1].

- **07–08/05/1915 (24–25/04 O.S.)**: German forces capture Liepāja and secure positions around the city[^1].

- **May–July 1915**: The front stabilizes roughly along the Pāvilosta–Skrunda–Venta line for more than two months[^1].

- **01/06/1915 (19/05 O.S.)**: Secret meeting in Riga between Latvian political, military, and public figures to discuss the creation of Latvian national military units within the Russian Army[^1].

- **10–11/06/1915 (28–29/05 O.S.)**: Heavy fighting near Leckava and Grieze. The Daugavgrīva Battalions suffer significant losses[^2].

- **29/06/1915 (16/06 O.S.)**: Russian military authorities order the evacuation of the civilian population from areas near the front in Courland[^4].

- **14/07/1915 (01/07 O.S.)**: New German offensive begins in Courland, aiming to outflank Russian positions around Šiauliai[^1].

- **14/07/1915 onward (01/07 O.S.)**: The Daugavgrīva Battalions conduct fighting withdrawals toward Riga[^2].

- **15/07/1915 (02/07 O.S.)**: Heavy fighting involving Latvian National Guard units around Jaunauce and Ķevele during the retreat from southern Courland[^5].

- **17/07/1915 (04/07 O.S.)**: German forces capture Dobele, Talsi, Tukums, and Ventspils during the offensive[^1].

- **17/07/1915 (04/07 O.S.)**: Jānis Goldmanis returns from the Northwestern Front headquarters with preliminary approval for the formation of Latvian military units[^6].

- **19/07/1915 (06/07 O.S.)**: Large-scale evacuation of Riga's industries begins. During the following month approximately 17,000 railway wagons of machinery, equipment, raw materials, and workers are evacuated eastward[^1].

- **19/07/1915 (06/07 O.S.)**: Russian military authorities formally approve the creation of Latvian Riflemen battalions[^6].

- **20–21/07/1915 (07–08/07 O.S.)**: The Daugavgrīva Battalions complete their withdrawal toward Riga. Units that had entered the campaign with roughly 2,000 men are reduced to approximately 500 effectives[^2].

- **31/07/1915 (18/07 O.S.)**: German forces capture Bauska[^1].

- **01/08/1915 (19/07 O.S.)**: German forces capture Jelgava[^1].

- **01/08/1915 (19/07 O.S.)**: Official order issued for the formation of the first two Latvian Riflemen battalions[^6].
