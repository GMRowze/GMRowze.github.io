A _Rowzeism_ is a repeated motif, phrase, expression, or idea that is either used in the writings of G.M. Rowze or by the community.

## 1. Expressions

* _**Get on the train**_: an expression for joining the community. A shameless derivative from the Greatful Dead fan vocabulary of being a fan on the band, which itself comes from [Ken Kesey's and the Merry Pranksters' bus (Furthur)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furthur_(bus)).


## 2. Quotes
* _**"If you wish to make an apple pie from scratch, you must first invent the universe":**_ a phrase by Carl Sagan in the 1980 television series "Cosmos". Often used to refer to the need of inventing more and more lore in backstories to support the current story, which can leading to a recursive paralysis of never finishing the current story.
* _**"It is absurd to divide people into good and bad. People are either charming or tedious:"**_ a quote by Oscar Wilde. Often used to refer to the idea that characters should be interesting, instead of simply divided into the author's morals.
* _**"The only thing worth writing about is the human heart in conflict with itself:"**_ a quote by William Faulkner in his 1950 Nobel Banquet Speech, also quoted by George R. R. Martin. 
* _**"\'Dwell on the past and you’ll lose an eye.' But the proverb goes on to say: 'Forget the past and you will lose both eyes'**_: Russian proverb cited by Aleksandr Solzhenitsyn in his novel "The Gulag Archipelago".
