### 2. Commitment against AI Slop

This archive does not use generative AI to automatically produce stories. The settings, themes, narratives, and characters published here are authored by me. That said, I am not particularly interested in performative technological purism either and allow myself to use AI tools to correct grammar or help with vocabulary. I believe that is a distinct use from outsourcing authorship and creativity.

I believe the main problem is not the AI itself bu the industrial production of soulless cultural "slop" optimized primarily for engagement and trends (see [this blog post](blog/02_why_write) for a longer discussion). On that aspect, I can promise that the stories published here will remain human-authored (and quite frankly, you will notice my writing quirks very quickly). You may also verify my writing progress and publication statistics above (or read [this post](blog/03_the_rowze_writing_system) regarding my writing system and publication transparency).

Likewise, unless deemed _necessary_ and _explicitly stated otherwise_, this archive will not use AI-generated images. As an author, I will prioritize either creating images myself, hiring designers (as I did with my first book, Latlander) or using properly credited public domain images (artwork, historical photographs, or illustrations).

### 2. Disclaimer on metafiction and in-universe texts

The Archive is intentionally structured in a way that blurs the boundary between fiction, historiography, literary criticism, archival reconstruction, and metafiction. For example, some works are written as though they were fictional memoirs, recovered manuscripts, annotated editions, translated correspondence, disputed historical documents, or texts published by the fictional in-universe editor G. M. Rowze himself. You can read a more in-depth explanation of this structure in [this blog post](blog/01_archivist_archive_rowzeverse).

### 4. Official Biography

G.M. Rowze is a Latvian-Brazilian folklorist, ethnographer, collector, publisher and writer. Having lived in Curitiba, he studied in France before the war, where he graduated with a Masters in Law, Economy and Management (*Droit, Économie et Gestion*) at the Université de Lille, in northern France. folklore, ethnography, historical fiction, action and mystery.
